# Scenarios (built and tested at A2; level is a setting)

## Scenario 1: Calling the huisarts

- **Partner role:** Receptionist (doktersassistente) at a GP practice
- **Learner goal:** Explain a health complaint and book an appointment
- **Key words:** afspraak, klachten, koorts, hoesten, wanneer, morgen
- **Opening line:** "Huisartsenpraktijk De Linde, goedemorgen. Waarmee kan ik u helpen?"
- **Scenario ends when:** An appointment day and time are agreed

## Scenario 2: Appointment at the gemeente

- **Partner role:** Desk employee (baliemedewerker) at the municipality
- **Learner goal:** Report a new address and book an appointment to register it
- **Key words:** inschrijven, adres, verhuizen, paspoort, huurcontract, afspraak
- **Opening line:** "Gemeente Rotterdam, goedemiddag. Waarmee kan ik u helpen?"
- **Scenario ends when:** An appointment is booked and the learner knows which documents to bring

## Scenario 3: Job interview

- **Partner role:** Recruiter at a company
- **Learner goal:** Introduce yourself, talk about your experience and explain why you want the job
- **Key words:** ervaring, werken, sterke punten, waarom, team, beschikbaar
- **Opening line:** "Goedemiddag, welkom! Fijn dat u er bent. Kunt u iets over uzelf vertellen?"
- **Scenario ends when:** The learner has answered 4 questions (introduction, experience, motivation, availability)
